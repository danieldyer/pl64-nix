How to read this replay

Drag the map to pan; scroll or use + / − to zoom. “Fit route” returns to the GNSS extent. “Follow” keeps the current estimate centred. Faint lines show the full route; brighter lines show the last 60 seconds.

The experimental replay uses the real estimator with gyro axes [y, −x, z], 60 s of coarse-alignment history, and correspondingly rotated gyro-noise densities. It starts afresh at the recording boundary. The original live estimate already had pre-recording history. Neither is independent ground truth; GNSS also feeds the filter.

Pitch is boresight elevation. Roll is rotation about the boresight, with right/down axes chosen as −packet X / +packet Y for the default −packet Z direction. Choosing +Z reverses forward and right. The map remains north-up. Boresight arrow length is schematic, not a distance.

States are exported at nominal 20 Hz. Playback interpolates position and quaternion attitude only between nearby samples in the same stage and segment; gaps and alignment changes are never interpolated across. Grey timeline marks denote missing IMU data. No filter output is smoothed or adjusted to match the roads.

Space: play/pause · ← / →: step 1 s · Shift + ← / →: step 10 s · Home/End: beginning/end · F: fit route. Shortcuts are inactive while a control has keyboard focus.
